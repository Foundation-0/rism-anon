## Method

- An incipit is the opening notes of a piece, as RISM records it. The script takes the first 12 pitches of each
  incipit and ignores rhythm.
- It keeps an incipit only if those 12 pitches include at least 5 different ones.
- It skips anonymous records that already cite a thematic catalogue (a catalogue that lists a composer's works by
  their opening notes).
- A match is an anonymous record whose first 12 pitches are exactly the same as those of attributed records, all by
  one composer.

minutes, and its output was identical to the saved output of the pilot, apart from the order in which it printed the
two genres tied at 108. The list of matched records was identical too.
